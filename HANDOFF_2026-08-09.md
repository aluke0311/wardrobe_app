## ⚠️ The most important caveat

**None of this has been verified against her real data.** The app can't
authenticate from localhost, so every measurement in these rounds came from
synthetic fixtures driven through the selftest harness's eval bridge. Her real
closet is ~476 items / ~1,543 outfits / ~4,000 wears; the fixtures are 150–450
items with generated outfit histories.

Where that matters most:

- **The proven-outfit yield.** Fixture outfits are generated from a repeating
  pattern, so their piece-overlap may be friendlier than reality. The direction
  (proof is abundant, the old bag couldn't hold it) is solid; the exact numbers
  are not a promise about her wardrobe.
- **`PACK_MODE_SPARE`** (light 0 · balanced 0.35 · flexible 0.8 × occasions) was
  tuned on fixtures. If Balanced feels wrong on a real trip, that constant is
  the dial — not the solver.
- **The unified trip screen is new and lightly exercised.** It composes existing
  renderers deliberately so the old views still work, but the first real trip
  through it is the actual test.

**First thing worth doing in a new session: ask how the last real trip went, and
be ready for the answer to invalidate a tuning constant rather than a design.**

## What got built

**The context-preference model** (`js/12-looks.js`) — `contextPref` /
`comboMeetsPrefs` / `effectivePrefs` / `prefsLabel` / `prefsPoolFilter`, kv
`"ctxprefs"`. Her rule about a kind of day. The trip-scoped override
(`packOccPref`, kv on the pack record) beats it for one occasion.

**Core / Spare** — `packCoreOptional`, `packApplyMode`, `packMode`. Replaced the
dial.

**The review** — `packReviewQueue` / `packReviewOptions` / `packReviewBeyond` /
`packAmbiguity` / `packOptionLabels` / `packChooseOutfit`, inline on occasion
cards plus a full screen (`renderPackOptionsPage`, `capsuleView "packopts"`).

**Proven outfits** — `packWornCandidates` (tier 1) / `packShapeCandidates`
(tier 2, reuses `formulaKeyFor`) / `packProvenSeed` / `packProvenBonus`.

**Scheduling** — `packOccDates` / `openPackMoveDaySheet`; pins honoured inside
`packSlate`'s placement.

**One trip screen** — `renderCapsuleTrip` / `tripPlanSectionHtml` /
`openTripMoreSheet`, `capsuleView "trip"`, dated trips only.

**The state seam** — `addItemsToCapsule` now writes the pack's piece list for all
ten of its callers; `packSyncMembers` passes `syncPack:false` as the projection.

Two new kv keys, no schema change: `"ctxprefs"`, `"ctxchoices"`.

## What is NOT built

Both were agreed and both are still open.

**1. Finalise — "I'm done packing."** A deterministic conflict check
(coverage · weather · laundry · exclusions · definites present · locks intact ·
dates valid) → "Ready to pack" or "1 issue to review", **never an automatic
repair**. Then the pack freezes and later edits are explicit. Plus a
what-changed trail.
⚠️ Freezing must stop **re-solving**, not the trip-mode features —
`packMidTripWash`, `tripUnwornNow` and `tripMissingPieces` legitimately change
while she's away.

**2. The learned-preference proposal.** *"You've picked separates for Dinner 5
times — stop offering dresses there?"* → writes `ctxprefs` on confirm.
Evidence has been accumulating in `kv "ctxchoices"` (contrastive: chose vs
against) since 2026-08-08 r4 and **nothing reads it yet**. Needs a real
threshold — every comparable derivation in this app has one.

Smaller, noted but not done: undo/reset coverage across the pack; DEFINITE and
EXCLUDED as first-class *visible* states with an unused-definite report ("hiking
boots — used in 0 outfits, keep anyway?"); "None of these" wired into the review
card.

## Process notes that earned their place this arc

These cost real time. They're in CLAUDE.md too, but they're what a new session
is most likely to repeat.

1. **Measure through the entry point she uses.** Driving `packSolve` directly
   gave 11/11/11; the app passes the filled bag and the truth was 8/8/9. The
   suite has `appSolve` for exactly this. I reported the wrong numbers before
   catching it.
2. **Render the screen and read it.** Three separate rounds found defects that
   were invisible in the markup and in green tests: "52 from your bag" over a
   list of 40; two tiles both labelled "What you have now"; three alternatives
   all labelled "Separates instead"; a 14px sideways scroll that predated the
   round.
3. **Mutation-check every new case, and watch for vacuity.** Two cases passed
   trivially on first write and survived their own mutations — the tier-ordering
   one only meant something with both tiers present.
4. **A guard that measures a proxy sends you to the wrong place.** The red
   lean/cushion case measured top-days when the decision was about outfits; it
   pointed at `repW`, which a mutation then exonerated.
5. **When a decision reverses, its guard reverses with it.** A red case is not
   automatically a regression.
6. **`TRIP_BUILDER.md`'s "do not re-litigate" has exceptions now** — D2 and D5
   are marked SUPERSEDED. Check a locked decision against the dated CLAUDE.md
   entries before treating it as binding.

## If you're picking this up cold

1. Baseline the suite before touching anything (`migration/selftest.html` at
   `http://localhost:4173/…`, via the `wardrobe` preview server). It has been
   green at 374; a red case on arrival is information, not necessarily yours.
2. Ask her how the last trip went before building. Two of the five rounds here
   existed only because the previous round's fix exposed the real problem
   underneath — her use is the instrument.
3. If the answer is "the bag is wrong", suspect a tuning constant
   (`PACK_MODE_SPARE`, `PACK_WORN_BONUS`) before the architecture.
4. If the answer is "it did something I didn't ask for", suspect a decision
   being silently reversed somewhere — that is the failure mode this whole
   rework has been correcting, and it has surfaced in a new place every round.
